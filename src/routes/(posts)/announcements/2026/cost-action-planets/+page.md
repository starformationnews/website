COST Action PLANETS is happy to share our YouTube channel [@COSTActionPLANETS](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCD8F4FEUg8GIwWufvzTZ2zA)! Our content includes seminars from two of our scientific working groups: Planet formation theory confronts observations and Emerging Habitable Environments; a code workshop series from our scientific working group Advancing planet formation models and an accessibility webinar from our Dissemination, Public Engagement and Outreach working group. Our content is aimed at anyone wanting to hear about advances in the planet-formation field, from PhD students to senior researchers. Please check it out and subscribe!
