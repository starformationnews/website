This month’s issue brings you 107 abstracts, a sign of how active our field is. It also raises a question we would like to answer together with you. 

The Star Formation Newsletter has always aimed to be a curated read for the star and planet formation communities, encouraging you to engage with the latest research but also to expose you to adjacent work. However, at this size it risks becoming a list that readers scroll past. We are thinking about changes that would make each issue easier to read and navigate, and that would better reflect the diversity of the work and the people in our community.

Before we decide anything, we want to hear from you. Please share your ideas in this [short form](https://forms.gle/AEVjuAnVwk3tzou56). Thank you for reading, and for helping us shape the future of the SFN.

The SFN Editors
